About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Dr. Fenglong Ma. I joined UIUC in Fall 2026 after doctoral studies in Informatics at Penn State, where I also received my M.S. I earned my B.E. in Big Data Management and Application from Peking University.

My research advances medical multimodal large language models, with an emphasis on grounded visual–textual understanding, fine-grained perception, and reliable reasoning. I have interned at GE HealthCare, building grounded and hallucination-resistant medical vision–language models, and at Amazon, working on language capabilities for generative recommendation.
